# Qualcomm / HUMAIN Horizon Ultra

## Summary

Horizon Ultra is a newly launched enterprise AI PC from HUMAIN built around Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Elite. Qualcomm and HUMAIN announced it on 2026-08-31, with enterprise availability beginning 2026-09-20. It is relevant to this catalog as a current OEM implementation of the Snapdragon X2 Elite platform, not as a new silicon architecture.

## Published hardware evidence

- SoC: Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Elite
- CPU: 18-core Qualcomm Oryon CPU (Horizon Ultra launch disclosure)
- NPU: Qualcomm Hexagon NPU; Qualcomm's current X2 Elite family page lists up to 80-85 TOPS depending on SKU
- Memory: device-specific; Qualcomm's X2 Elite product brief lists up to 128 GB LPDDR5X and 152 GB/s for the relevant 18-core family configurations; Horizon Ultra-specific capacity was not disclosed in the launch release
- GPU: integrated Qualcomm Adreno GPU; exact Horizon Ultra SKU and GPU throughput were not disclosed
- Storage, cooling, sustained power and networking: not disclosed in the launch release

## Software/runtime

Qualcomm positions Snapdragon X2 Elite for Windows 11 Copilot+ PCs and on-device AI. The launch release does not provide a model-specific LLM runtime, throughput benchmark, or power measurement for Horizon Ultra. Qualcomm's broader X2 Elite ecosystem should be treated as platform-level support until an OEM configuration and reproducible benchmark are available.

## Availability and pricing

- Availability: enterprise purchase beginning 2026-09-20, according to Qualcomm/HUMAIN
- Public price: not disclosed
- Region: launch announced in Riyadh; enterprise sales scope beyond the initial launch was not specified

## Evidence quality and research gaps

- Manufacturer: launch date, platform and CPU configuration
- Manufacturer/platform: X2 Elite memory ceiling, bandwidth and NPU TOPS
